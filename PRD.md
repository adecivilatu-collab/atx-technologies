## 1. Project Overview

ATX Technologies is a technology company focused on helping individuals and businesses adopt modern technology, improve productivity, and grow through digital innovation.

The company website will serve as its professional online presence, showcasing its services, attracting customers, building trust, and generating business enquiries.

The website will launch initially as a corporate website, with room to expand into technology training, AI solutions, software products, and other digital services.

* **Brand name:** ATX Technologies  
* **Full meaning:** Advanced Technology Xcellence  
* **Brand positioning:** AI-driven innovation, practical technology solutions, and digital transformation.

---

## 2. Project Objectives

The website should:

1. Establish a professional and credible online identity for ATX Technologies.
2. Showcase the company's four core service areas.
3. Generate enquiries from individuals, startups, and established businesses.
4. Make it easy for visitors to contact the company through WhatsApp and email.
5. Introduce visitors to AI and automation solutions.
6. Provide an initial rule-based chatbot to answer frequently asked questions.
7. Create a foundation for future technology training, digital products, and advanced AI services.

## 3. Target Audience

The website will serve:

* Small and medium-sized businesses seeking technology solutions.
